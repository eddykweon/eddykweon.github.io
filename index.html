The Light Engine is a Korean video briefing on AR/VR/MR hardware, centred on microdisplays (OLEDoS, LCoS, microLED) and the near-eye optics they drive. Each weekday episode summarises public news and filings in our own words, with every source credited.

About the uploader app

“The Light Engine Uploader” is a small program run by the channel’s owner. It uploads the videos we produce to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It has one user, the channel owner, and does not access anyone else’s account or data.

  • It uses Google sign-in (OAuth). The channel owner approves access once; no password is stored.
  • It only uploads videos and sets their title, description and visibility on our channel.
  • It collects no information about viewers or any other people.
