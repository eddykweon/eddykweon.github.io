Privacy Policy

This policy covers “The Light Engine Uploader” (the “app”), a program operated by the owner of The Light Engine YouTube channel to upload that channel’s own videos.

Who uses the app

The app has a single user: the owner of The Light Engine channel. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube account access. Through Google OAuth, the channel owner authorises the app to upload and manage videos on The Light Engine channel. The app uses YouTube API Services only for that purpose.
  • Access token. Google issues an access/refresh token, which is stored only on the channel owner’s own computer and is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • Our own videos and their metadata (title, description, visibility), which we create.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process information about viewers or any other people.
  • It does not read comments, subscriber lists, analytics or other users’ content.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It uses no cookies, tracking or advertising identifiers. This website sets no cookies either.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the stored token on the owner’s computer also ends access.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app’s use of data changes; the date above shows the latest version. For questions, please use the contact options on our YouTube channel’s About page.
